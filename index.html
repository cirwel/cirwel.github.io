Runtime infrastructure for persistent AI agents.

I build systems for agent work that spans sessions, restarts, and handoffs. The main project, UNITARES, keeps identity, evidence, memory, runtime state, review, and coordination accountable across those boundaries.

Persistent describes continuity of the work and its accountable record — not a claim that one process runs forever. UNITARES sits alongside model providers and agent frameworks rather than replacing them.

§04 — Experiments

What is actually being asked.

The stack is also a research apparatus. These questions matter more than raw event volume — and two of the three are not settled.

Can runtime state discriminate?

Do recorded operational patterns meaningfully differ across agents or workload classes?

Can it predict?

Does prior state anticipate meaningful outcomes better than simpler baselines? This remains unresolved.

Does acting on it help?

Intervention benefit is not established. External or adversarial evaluation is still needed.

Dogfood

UNITARES is used in the development of UNITARES, through attributed memory, advisory consultation, review, check-ins, and handoffs.

Authority separation

Consultation and review add evidence; they do not automatically become decision authority.

Single-operator disclosure

The production deployment demonstrates sustained use of the mechanisms in one environment, not cross-operator generality.
