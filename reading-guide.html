Four papers on making long-running AI agent work inspectable across sessions, changing runtimes, and separately controlled systems.

Orientation

What EISV records

EISV is intended to help agents read their runtime state and operators inspect the system. Its implemented coordinates compress and smooth observations, including the controller’s own history:

E
A blend of prior policy actions, controller coherence level, complexity divergence, and available outcome, continuity, and tool features.
I
A calibration and consistency blend. The deployed calibration input is fleet-scoped; a reading is not a correctness score for the current claim.
S
A blend of drift, regime changes, complexity divergence, and available continuity and tool features.
V
The separately smoothed, signed gap between the raw E and I observations.

Controller feedback remains information about the system’s response. It does not independently confirm the condition that prompted that response. Measurement fidelity, diagnosis, usefulness to a reader, outcome prediction, and intervention benefit are distinct claims. A signal can be recorded and inspected while those further questions remain open.

01 · Instrument audit

A Flat Coherence Score

What information did one deployed score retain, and what did it contribute at the thresholds where it was used?

Evidence
Two frozen production windows and a reproducible basin-label replay. The legacy coherence score stayed concentrated and rarely affected those basin assignments, while retaining descriptive class information. A per-class replacement changed labels.
Still open
Whether the replacement measures the intended property better, helps a particular reader or task, or merits a policy change. The replay establishes a change in classification; it does not validate the replacement labels.

02 · Framework

UNITARES

How can runtime observations support state estimation and governance across heterogeneous agents?

Evidence
A mathematical framework, dated deployment measurements, calibration analyses, and an explicitly bounded continuous-system contraction result. The current implementation uses weighted indices; the information-theoretic definitions remain research targets.
Still open
The empirical interpretation and usefulness of particular estimates. The continuous proof does not transfer automatically to the numerical update or adaptive governor. Use the runtime definitions above for the present implementation; the paper describes its stated deployment period.

Read v6.9.4 · Source, figures, and audits

03 · Identity

Trajectory Identity

Can patterns over time describe continuity beyond a reused identifier?

Evidence
A trajectory-signature framework, observations from one embodied agent, a stored twelve-episode recovery summary, and a four-agent pilot. A separate production-metric audit exposed sensitivity to era and harness. That audit and the pilot use different instruments.
Still open
Individual discrimination across harness changes, separation from role, and independent reconstruction of recovery from raw history. The paper’s five scored components and derived Eta summary must be distinguished from the separate UNITARES implementation.

Read v0.18 · Source and analyses

04 · Accountability

Accountability Without
a Trusted Center

How can separately controlled systems establish who acted, under whose authority, and through which delegation chain?

Evidence
A testbed design and frozen evaluation plan, a single-server trace, and an eleven-case attestation demonstration across two hosts. One operator controlled both hosts.
Still open
The registered comparison of federated and centralized control, authorization baselines, and independent replication. The attestation demonstration exercises a primitive; it supplies no benchmark result for federated governors.

Companion evidence

Instrument quality has its own tests

Recent runtime work adds evidence separate from the paper replay. An exploratory synthetic S-axis experiment found responses to injected deviations and reproduced identical readings for identical input and initial state. It also exposed vocabulary sensitivity and a regime feedback loop. An E/I input audit identified controller-history dependence and missing admissible calibration pairs. These findings guide instrument repair and evaluation.

Faithful response, robustness, and usable explanations can be studied directly. Outcome prediction is a separate registered question, and intervention authority requires a separate decision.
